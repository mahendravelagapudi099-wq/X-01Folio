## Internship Experience at Codec Technologies

From May to June 2025, I worked as a remote Data Science Intern at Codec Technologies Pvt. Ltd. During this period, my primary responsibility was contributing to the development of VectorMind, an internal sales-analytics platform.

## What VectorMind Does

VectorMind was designed to help non-technical stakeholders interrogate enterprise sales datasets effortlessly. Its core functions include:

• Explaining sales trends across quarters
• Generating future sales forecasts from historical patterns
• Answering natural-language questions about commercial data
• Visualizing breakdowns through an interactive Streamlit interface

> VectorMind: a sales-analytics platform built during a Data Science internship at Codec Technologies Pvt. Ltd (May–Jun 2025, Remote). It explains trends, forecasts sales, and answers natural-language questions about sales data.

## Technical Lessons & Takeaways

Working on VectorMind emphasized the importance of data sanity and conversational clarity. In commercial analytics, providing an accurate, grounded answer with clear boundaries is far more valuable than speculative forecasting.
